Privacy Policy

Book Bible · Effective November 21, 2026

Book Bible does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your highlights, notes, notebook pages and drawings, liked verses, prayer cards and other keepsakes, reading place and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you use iCloud backup, they are included in your device backup under Apple's own terms.

Photos

If you choose a photo for your Bible's cover, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. Only the photo you pick is copied into the app, on your device. The app cannot see the rest of your library.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily verse reminder, the reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Cover Collection is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
