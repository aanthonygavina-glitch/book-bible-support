Common questions

How do I highlight? Press and drag across words (or run Apple Pencil over them). Tap a verse for Highlight, or use More → Highlighter for every color.

Where are my notes? The Notes tab: Notebook, Highlights and Tucked in. Tap New note to write with Apple Pencil or type.

How do I see the Greek or Hebrew? Tap a verse, then Greek or Hebrew. Tap any word for its meaning.

I bought the Cover Collection on another device. Open More → Settings → Restore purchase.

Does it need the internet? No. All four translations, search, cross references and the Greek and Hebrew work offline.
